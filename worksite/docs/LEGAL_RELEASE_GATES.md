# Before public release — verification gates

- Check Arizona Corporation Commission status of VerbaEdge Systems LLC. Verify owner authority.
- Confirm DBA selection and availability at Arizona Secretary of State; obtain acceptance/certificate before public legal claims where applicable.
- Check USPTO and common-law marks, similar names, and desired domain ownership.
- Confirm actual business address, contact email and applicable privacy notice.
- Avoid claiming an LLC exists under a trade name; a trade name is the same legal entity.
- Do not sell or advertise for-hire motor-carrier, freight-broker, freight-forwarder or customs-broker services without checking all relevant US federal/state approvals. CDL is a driver credential, not entity authority.
- For maintenance contracting check Arizona ROC, electrical, HVAC, fire work, insurance, EPA Section 608, OSHA LOTO and worker qualifications before offering physical work.
- Ensure supplier and subcontractor contracts; no claims of staff or partners without verification.
- Record per-image provenance, reuse rights, applicable releases, and required attribution before adding photography or publishing inherited illustrations. The import's candidate photo references are in [Image and UI design provenance](IMAGE_RIGHTS_AND_DESIGN.md).
- Use real customer testimonials, KPIs and certifications only with evidence and permission.
- Production demo requires access control, privacy assessment, audit logs, retention rules, monitoring, backup/recovery and authorized data rights.
- Secure DNS and email: take full DNS export and preserve MX/SPF/DKIM/DMARC before cutover.
- Confirm forms and analytics privacy obligations before turning on data collection.
