## Intelloop (https://www.intel-loop.com/)
Its published service structure includes: industrial/asset launch planning (https://www.intel-loop.com/adapt-launch), maintenance/reliability review (https://www.intel-loop.com/adaptive-excel), OT/digital integration (https://www.intel-loop.com/addaptive-tech), facilities management (https://www.intel-loop.com/facilities-management) and Atlas CMMS context (https://www.intel-loop.com/atlas-cmms). It also advertises energy/EV/battery work. Its quantified outcomes, methods, clients and case studies belong to Intelloop and MUST NOT be reproduced as VerbaEdge results.

## Atlas CMMS (https://atlas-cmms.com/)
Official public product materials describe asset registers, maintenance requests, work orders, preventive schedules, inventory, mobile, reports and dashboards. Source repo: https://github.com/coocookanoo/cmms. The repo describes AGPLv3 open-source AND a separate commercial licence for white-label/custom-branded and advanced features. This starter kit DOES NOT redistribute or integrate Atlas source and does not brand itself as Atlas. If management later decides to adopt Atlas, obtain a licence review and compare self-host vs commercial terms.

## VerbaEdge Ops original service system (proposal)
1. **Discover**: workflow inventory, asset criticality, regulatory/credential register, exception logs.
2. **Operate**: intake, dispatch planning, PM scheduling, warehouse/facility work and fleet-readiness documentation.
3. **Integrate**: CMMS-style original schema, partner-authorized API, data provenance and management reporting.
4. **Improve**: maintenance backlog, response times, preventive compliance, failure analyses and control reviews.
5. **TradeFlow**: handoff and document readiness for shipments, border-port references, invoice/PO matching and exceptional-event audit.
