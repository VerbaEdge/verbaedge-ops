# VerbaEdge Ops redesign research

Reviewed November 27, 2026. This brief records what the businesses publish about their own offerings. Vendor performance figures, customer stories, and support commitments have not been independently substantiated. The redesign uses original editorial copy and workflows scoped to the VerbaEdge project.

## Source boundaries and implementation choices

The uploaded Ops archive already names OpsCare, uses TradeFlow for freight-information workflows, and includes a browser-local demonstration. Its CMMS blueprint distinguishes the public site from future authenticated tools. Public language focuses on planning records and operational decisions. Product roadmaps, contractor qualifications, customer results and live telemetry require independent evidence before becoming public capability claims. Robert's CDL is an owner-supplied biographical fact and conveys no evidence of corporate transportation authority.
