## Trade name
- Arizona Secretary of State says trade names (DBAs) are generally not legally mandatory, but accepted practice: https://azsos.gov/business/tntm
- Register as the LLC owner under the exact ACC legal name. State filing is NOT trademark clearance and does not create a new LLC. Fee is shown by current AZ SOS outreach material as $10 standard plus optional $25 expedite, but verify the live checkout: https://azsos.gov/business/mbs
- Search trade names and trademarks, US federal USPTO database, and domain before design expenditures. Owner approval required for filings, bank updates or new domain purchases.

## Freight and CDL
- A personal CDL shows qualification to drive certain commercial vehicles, depending on class and endorsements; it does NOT create USDOT registration, for-hire motor-carrier authority, property broker authority or freight forwarder authorization for VerbaEdge Systems LLC.
- FMCSA broker requirements: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/broker-registration — federal registration, BMC-84 surety or BMC-85 trust of $75,000, BOC-3 and other filings. Financial responsibility rules updated 2026.
- Motor carrier authority and insurance requirements vary by operating model, interstate/intrastate, vehicle weight and cargo; verify with FMCSA and Arizona authorities before publishing operational offerings.
- Customs brokers conducting U.S. customs business for others generally must be licensed: https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1390. Importer self-entry differs from third-party brokerage.
- Do not claim vessels, port access, freight brokerage, trucks, drivers, 24/7 dispatch or 3PL services from this kit.

## Maintenance and facilities
- Arizona ROC scope and contractor licence classifications: https://roc.az.gov/license-classifications and https://roc.az.gov/license-classifications-page. Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire-suppression and other works can require licensed contracting entities; scope is fact-specific.
- OSHA lockout/tagout: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.147. A digital checklist is not a substitute for site energy-control procedures and qualified employees.
- EPA refrigerant technician certification: https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification. Does not authorize company-level contracting without other applicable requirements.
- Validate insurance, workers compensation, employment and subcontracting before physical facilities work.
