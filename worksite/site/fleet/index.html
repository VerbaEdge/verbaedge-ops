Plan the next service before the next load.

Vehicle and trailer register

Bring unit identifiers, service history, mileage or engine hours and inspection due dates into one planning record.

Defect to work order

Log the driver’s observation, vehicle and time. Route it to the responsible reviewer and record their decision before updating the dispatch handover.

Preventive service

Schedule work against dates, mileage or hours. Track the workshop slot, parts availability, completion evidence and next interval.

Driver and partner records

Track document owners, review dates and expiry reminders. Keep credential verification with the authorized reviewer.

Dispatch and appointment handover

Link the load reference, equipment assignment, driver contact and appointment window. Flag changes and record who acknowledged the handoff.

Cost and utilization review

Compare mileage, fuel, downtime and work-order costs by unit. Preserve the measurement period and source behind each figure.
