Operations planning

Give every shift a clear handover.

Connect the daily work across sites, crews and contractors. Start with one recurring delay and trace it from request to closeout.

What the work covers

Maintenance operationsFacilitiesFleet readinessCMMS design
Intake and escalation

Give each request a site, priority, owner and response window. Separate urgent exceptions from work that can wait for the next planning meeting.

Contractor coordination

Define the job scope, access window, required permits and acceptance criteria. Keep site approval and contractor sign-off with the record.

Service and purchasing

Connect a parts request to the job, quote, purchase order and delivery date. The planner can see which jobs are waiting on materials.

Shift and site reviews

Review open work, blocked jobs and unresolved risks. Pass the next team a short action list with named owners and due dates.

Backlog and performance

Agree how to count overdue work, time to close and planned versus reactive jobs. Review the oldest blockers before setting new targets.
