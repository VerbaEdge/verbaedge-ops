Maintenance services

What needs attention?

Start with the work that keeps slipping. Open a service to see what your team can put in place.

Work orders & shift handoff

Turn a reported fault into a job with an asset ID, priority, owner and target date. Keep blocked work visible through shift changes and record what the next person needs to know.

Planning output A work queue with assignment, hold reason, closeout evidence and follow-up.

Preventive maintenance

Build a calendar from owner-approved service intervals, equipment hours or mileage. Match each job to a service window, required skills and available parts.

Planning output A weekly service schedule with due, overdue and deferred tasks.

Facilities & service vendors

Group building systems by site and location. Coordinate access, service windows and site contacts for HVAC, utility equipment, loading docks and building inspections.

Planning output A site service calendar with vendor scope, access contact and acceptance owner.

Fleet & equipment readiness

Bring vehicle and trailer records, reported defects and service appointments into one review. Record the qualified reviewer’s release decision for each unit.

Planning output A readiness list showing availability, open defects and the next decision.

Parts & purchasing

Connect each critical spare to its equipment, supplier reference and bin. Flag shortages before scheduling and reconcile issued or returned parts when the job closes.

Planning output A job kit list with quantities on hand, reservations and reorder triggers.

Asset launch & handover

Give new or transferred equipment a home in the register. Collect nameplate IDs, manuals, warranties, initial readings and service tasks before ownership changes.

Planning output A handover pack with an owner and a due date for every missing item.

CMMS setup & data handoff

Map site, asset and part IDs to the fields in your computerized maintenance management system. Test a sample import and walk a request from intake through acceptance with the people who will use it.

Planning output A field map, import exceptions, role assignments and pilot acceptance checks.

Reliability & backlog review

Review repeat faults, aging jobs, missed service windows and parts holds. Agree which event starts and ends downtime before comparing records across sites.

Planning output A review sheet that connects each recurring issue to an action, owner and review date.
