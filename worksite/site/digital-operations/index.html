Maintenance technology

Build the record around the equipment.

Design a CMMS workflow around the assets, parts and decisions your team already manages. Test it on a small asset group before expanding.

What the work covers

Maintenance operationsOperations planningFacilitiesFleet readiness
Asset hierarchy and migration

Group assets by site, system and equipment. Map existing spreadsheet columns, resolve duplicate identifiers and check a sample against the equipment register.

Work orders and preventive tasks

Define the fields needed from issue intake through closeout. Set priority, assignee, trigger and completion evidence for each recurring task.

Parts and contractor access

Link parts use to a work order. Define who can request, approve and issue stock, and which job records a contractor may see.

Field access and integrations

Plan mobile forms around the technician’s visit. Define source ownership, update frequency and behavior when connections or meter readings are missing.

Reporting and pilot acceptance

Choose one operational question, such as which assets account for repeated downtime. Test the report against sample records with the planner who will use it.
