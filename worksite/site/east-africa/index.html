NORTHERN CORRIDOR · COUNTRY & ROUTE COVERAGE

From Mombasa through Uganda to Kigali, with regional branches.

The Northern Corridor connects the Port of Mombasa to inland destinations across the Great Lakes region. VerbaEdge's proposed software workflows can be configured for Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda, while considering the wider corridor network to Burundi, eastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, and South Sudan. Coverage here describes a planning scope, not live operations or verified integrations.

01 · Kenya

Mombasa → Nairobi → Malaba or Busia

Structure export/import documentation and shipment milestones around Port of Mombasa origin records, inland handoffs and the selected Kenya–Uganda border crossing. Nakuru and Eldoret are relevant waypoints on the Malaba route.

02 · Uganda

Malaba or Busia → Jinja → Kampala

Track authorized carrier updates, broker handoffs, document exceptions and onward movement. The Uganda leg provides connections toward Rwanda and other Northern Corridor destinations.

03 · Rwanda

Kampala → Gatuna or Kagitumba → Kigali

Include Rwanda-bound consignments, using the Katuna/Gatuna crossing or the Mirama Hills/Kagitumba crossing as appropriate to the operator's route. Track document responsibilities, border events and Kigali delivery confirmation.

Network branches

Burundi · Eastern DRC · South Sudan

The official Northern Corridor network also serves these destinations. They are wider-network context and future workflow-discovery candidates, not currently claimed VerbaEdge deployments or an assertion that all branches follow the Kigali route.

Scope and evidence

Configurable handoffs, not customs filing

Crossing selection, permits, document requirements and customs workflows vary by cargo and jurisdiction. Actual routes, datasets, broker integrations and regulatory requirements must be confirmed for each pilot; no automated clearance, tracking feed or authorized filings are implied.

Route references: East African Community road transport network and Northern Corridor Transit and Transport Coordination Authority.

For teams managing cross-border freight

Make the next handoff easier.

Shipping records, driver updates, clearance handoffs and customer communications often arrive through different channels. A clear operational record helps teams see what is ready, what has changed and who needs to act.

Exceptions and billing

Resolve outstanding items

Assign missing details, clarify invoice and order references, and keep a record of follow-ups until they are closed.
